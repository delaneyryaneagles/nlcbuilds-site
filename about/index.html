30 years on the tools. One company built on keeping our word.

Ryan spent decades learning the trade from people who taught him to do things properly. In 2023, Ryan and Katie started Next Level Construct on a simple idea: the person who quotes your project should be the person standing behind it. In 2026 the company became NLC Builds: same team, same licenses, sharper name.

We've grown by word of mouth, one homeowner telling the next. Today we work across Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware, and we still run every job the same way: honest written pricing, clean work, and finishing what we start. There's no sales rep in between, which is why the estimate matches what gets built.

For condominium associations and property managers, we also run balcony and railing programs with per-unit documentation.
