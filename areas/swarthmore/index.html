Homes in Swarthmore

We build for the house you actually have.

Much of Swarthmore's housing dates from the Victorian period through the 1920s, built as rail service turned the college town into a commuter suburb. Many still have their deep porches, wood windows and original trim.

These are 100-year-old houses. We work carefully around plaster and old framing, and we keep the details that make them Swarthmore.
