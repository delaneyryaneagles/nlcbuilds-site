Homes in Moorestown

We build for the house you actually have.

Moorestown goes back to 1682, and its Main Street historic area has more than 350 contributing buildings, from Victorian and Second Empire to Revival styles. Most of the township's homes, though, are mid-century and later, and many are ready for new kitchens, finished basements and better outdoor space.

The Township's own permit guides call finished basements 'a popular home improvement project.' We build them, permit and all.

Questions from Moorestown homeowners
Good to know.
Do I need a permit to finish my basement in Moorestown?

Yes. Zoning approval plus construction permits with plans. We handle the whole process.

Is my Moorestown home under the new Historic Preservation Commission?

The HPC, created in 2025, reviews exterior changes in the designated district, which centers on Main Street and Chester Avenue. We confirm your property's status before design.
