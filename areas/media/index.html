Homes in Media & Upper Providence

We build for the house you actually have.

Permits & approvals
We handle the paperwork.

Every project is permitted, inspected and closed out properly. Here's what applies in Media & Upper Providence:

Media Borough · Upper Providence Twp · Middletown Twp
Official permit info →
  • Media Borough: in its historic districts, Borough Council issues a COA (on HARB's advice) before exterior work visible from the street.
  • Upper Providence: permits are required before adding a deck or an extension. Sealed plans for additions; separate stormwater permit.
  • Middletown: online applications only (GovWell). We file them.
Questions from Media & Upper Providence homeowners
Good to know.
Does my Media home need historic approval?

Only if it's in the Courthouse Square, Lemon Street or Providence Friends' Meeting House districts, and only for exterior work visible from the street. We check your address first.

Do I need a permit for a deck in Upper Providence?

Yes. The Township asks for a permit before adding a deck or extension. We handle it.
