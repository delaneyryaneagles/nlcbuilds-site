Projects that fit Mullica Hill & Woolwich homes.

Basement finishing →

Harrison's basement code checklist covers pressure-treated bottom plates, fire-blocking, smoke alarms, and bedroom egress windows (5 sq ft minimum opening, sill no higher than 44 in). We build to every item.

Decks & porches →

Permits & approvals
We handle the paperwork.

Every project is permitted, inspected and closed out properly. Here's what applies in Mullica Hill & Woolwich:

Harrison Twp Construction Office (114 Bridgeton Pike) · Woolwich Twp Construction Office (120 Village Green Dr)
Official permit info →
  • Harrison: additions, decks and porches start with zoning, then UCC permits (filed in person or by mail). A final inspection happens before your final payment.
  • Harrison's historic Mullica Hill district along Main Street needs Historic Preservation Commission approval for exterior work.
  • Harrison basements: grinder/ejector pumps must be cleared with the Gloucester County Health Dept., since many lots are on septic. Woolwich: siding, decks, basements and additions need a call to the Construction Office first.

Questions from Mullica Hill & Woolwich homeowners
Good to know.
What does it take to finish a basement in Harrison Township?

A construction permit and a build that meets the Township's checklist: treated bottom plates, fire-blocking, smoke alarms, outlet spacing, and a proper egress window for any bedroom. We handle every item.

Do I need zoning approval for a deck in Mullica Hill?

Yes. In Harrison, decks and porches start with a zoning application, then the construction permit. We file both.
