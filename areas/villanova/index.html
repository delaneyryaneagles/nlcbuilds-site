Homes in Villanova

We build for the house you actually have.

Villanova straddles two townships and two counties: Radnor in Delaware County and Lower Merion in Montgomery County. Your street decides whose permits, stormwater rules and review boards apply. Get that wrong and a project can stall for months.

The area grew from gentlemen's farms into stone-built estates at the end of the 19th century. We work at that standard: careful demolition, framing that's square and level, and finish carpentry that holds up to close inspection.

Permits & approvals
We handle the paperwork.

Every project is permitted, inspected and closed out properly. Here's what applies in Villanova:

Radnor Township or Lower Merion Township (depends on your address)
Official permit info →
  • First step on every Villanova job: confirm whether you're in Radnor (Delaware Co.) or Lower Merion (Montgomery Co.).
  • Radnor: Community Development issues permits; grading at ~500 SF and a stormwater plan at 1,000 SF of new hard surface.
  • Lower Merion: Building & Planning in Ardmore; additions need an impervious-surface calculation, and adding 1,500 SF or less is exempt from peak-rate stormwater control.

Villanova is split between Radnor Township and Lower Merion Township. We look up your parcel before design so we follow the right rules from day one.
