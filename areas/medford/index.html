Homes in Medford & Medford Lakes

We build for the house you actually have.

Most of Medford sits in the New Jersey Pinelands, about 78% of the township, on wooded lots around lakes like Taunton, Birchwood and Lake Pine. Medford Lakes, founded in the late 1920s, is known for its 22 lakes and its log cabins, which make up more than 10% of the borough's homes.

Much of the township was built in the 1970s through the '90s, so many homes are ready for new kitchens, windows and outdoor living.

Permits & approvals
We handle the paperwork.

Every project is permitted, inspected and closed out properly. Here's what applies in Medford & Medford Lakes:

Medford Township Construction & Inspections (49 Union St.)
Official permit info →
  • UCC permits are required for additions and renovations, and Medford requires HIC registration with every residential permit. Ours is 13VH14325000.
  • Pinelands: about 78% of the township is in the state Pinelands Area, where some development needs a Pinelands Commission filing. We check before design.
  • Lake communities may have their own HOA or lake-club approvals. We coordinate them. Medford Lakes is a separate borough with its own offices.
