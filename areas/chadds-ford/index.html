Homes in Chadds Ford & Thornbury

We build for the house you actually have.

The next addition should look like it was always there. We match rooflines, siding, stone and trim so the old and new read as one house.

Permits & approvals
We handle the paperwork.

Every project is permitted, inspected and closed out properly. Here's what applies in Chadds Ford & Thornbury:

Chadds Ford Township · Thornbury Township (Delaware Co.) · Birmingham Township
Official permit info →
  • Chadds Ford: the historic district runs along Baltimore Pike from Brandywine Creek. HARB advises the Supervisors, and a Certificate of Appropriateness comes before the building permit.
  • Birmingham: the historic district along Birmingham Road (Dilworthtown) works the same way, with the COA first.
  • Thornbury (Delco): a zoning permit and plot plan go with every building permit. Note that there are two Thornbury Townships; we confirm which one is yours.
Questions from Chadds Ford & Thornbury homeowners
Good to know.
Do I need historic approval for a porch in Chadds Ford?

If your home is in the township's historic district, yes. A Certificate of Appropriateness is required before the building permit. We prepare drawings and present to HARB.

Can you match an old stone farmhouse?

Yes. We plan rooflines, stone or siding, and window patterns so an addition belongs to the original house.
