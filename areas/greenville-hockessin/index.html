Official permit info →
  • Greenville, Centreville and Hockessin are unincorporated, so the County issues permits through eApply/ePlans.
  • Kitchens: replacing cabinets and countertops needs no permit. Moving walls or plumbing, or creating or relocating windows and doors, does.
  • Historic overlay parcels need Historic Review Board approval before any exterior permit. We check your parcel's zoning.
