How we build it

Decks & Porches, done the way it should be.

Decks and porches are the same trade: footings, framing, a floor system, railings and stairs, built to carry weight and shed water for decades. The part that matters is the part you never see once it's done, so that's where we spend the time.

We build new decks, rebuild worn-out ones, replace boards and railings, and restore or rebuild front porches on older homes, from Main Line Victorians to Haddonfield colonials. Composite or wood decking, with cable, metal or wood railings, and porch floors, columns and trim that match the house.

Permits & approvals
The rules change town to town.

We pull the permits and handle historic review. A few rules that catch homeowners out:

  • Haverford Twp — Every deck needs a permit, and the contractor must be licensed with the township.
  • Lower Merion — A deck of 200 SF or less doesn't count against your lot's impervious limit; above that, half of it counts.
  • Haddonfield — Porches are a focus of the Historic Preservation Commission: repair before replace.
  • Chadds Ford & Birmingham — Historic approval (COA) comes before the building permit.
  • Easttown — A covered porch may also need a drainage permit.
  • New Castle County, DE — Only small detached decks (200 SF or less, under 30" high) skip a permit.
Decks & Porches questions
Good to know.
Are decks and porches the same kind of project?

Structurally, yes: footings, framing, a floor and railings. A porch usually adds a roof and finish details that match the front of the house. We build both.

Do you work with composite decking?

Yes, composite and wood decking, with cable, metal or wood railings.

Do I need a permit for a new deck?

Almost always. Haverford Township requires one for every deck, and most towns we serve do too. We pull the permit and schedule the inspections.

Can you repair a historic porch instead of replacing it?

Yes. In historic districts like Haddonfield the commission prefers repair, and we rebuild only the parts that can't be saved.

Do you work with HOAs?

Yes. We run multi-unit deck, balcony and railing programs for HOAs, condo associations and property managers.
