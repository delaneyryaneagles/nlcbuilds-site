How we build it

Windows & Doors, done the way it should be.

Most window and door problems aren't the product. They're the opening: rot behind the old frame, no flashing, a sill that slopes the wrong way. We open it up, fix what's there, and set the new unit square and plumb before it's sealed and trimmed.

We replace single windows and whole houses, swap fogged basement hoppers, and install entry doors, sliding patio doors, storm doors and interior doors with finish carpentry to match. On older homes in historic districts we work with the review board on what's allowed.

Permits & approvals
The rules change town to town.

We pull the permits and handle historic review. A few rules that catch homeowners out:

  • Haddonfield — The historic commission expects wood windows in the district, not vinyl or clad.
  • Tredyffrin — A same-size window swap doesn't need a permit.
  • Media borough — Three historic districts: street-visible exterior changes need a certificate of appropriateness.
  • Radnor — North Wayne, South Wayne and Louella Court are historic (HARB) districts.
  • Lower Merion — Seven HARB districts, including Gladwyne and Merion Square.
  • Harrison Twp, NJ — A basement egress window needs at least 5 sq ft of opening with the sill no higher than 44".
Windows & Doors questions
Good to know.
Can you replace just one window or door?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we'll give you an honest written estimate.

What kinds of doors do you install?

Entry doors, sliding patio doors, storm doors and interior doors, with the trim finished to match.

Can I put vinyl windows on a house in a historic district?

It depends on the town. Haddonfield's commission expects wood windows in the district. We check the rules for your street before you choose a product.

Do window replacements need a permit?

Sometimes. In Tredyffrin a same-size swap doesn't; resizing an opening or adding egress usually does. We handle whatever your town requires.
