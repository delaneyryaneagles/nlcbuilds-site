How we build it

Additions & Dormers, done the way it should be.

An addition has to match the house: rooflines, trim, siding, window style. When it's done well, nobody can tell where the old house stops. We frame it, close it in, and finish it to that standard.

Additions also carry the most paperwork: zoning setbacks, stormwater, historic review and building plans. We plan for all of it up front so the schedule holds.

Permits & approvals
The rules change town to town.

We pull the permits and handle historic review. A few rules that catch homeowners out:

  • Radnor — About 500 SF of new impervious surface triggers a grading plan; 1,000 SF needs a full stormwater plan.
  • Chadds Ford & Birmingham — Historic approval comes before the building permit.
  • Edgmont — Starting work before the permit is issued doubles the fees.
  • Newtown Twp (Delco) — The permit application needs the contract and the contractor's PA HIC insurance certificate.
  • New Jersey — Plans come from an NJ design professional; home improvement contractors can't prepare them.
Additions & Dormers questions
Good to know.
Do you design additions?

We plan the project with you and work with a design professional for the drawings where your town or state requires sealed plans.

Will the addition match my house?

That's the goal. Rooflines, trim, siding and windows are matched so it looks original.

How long do permits take for an addition?

It depends on the town and whether zoning or historic review is involved. We map it out before you sign so there are no surprises.
