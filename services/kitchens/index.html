Permits & approvals
The rules change town to town.

We pull the permits and handle historic review. A few rules that catch homeowners out:

  • Tredyffrin — Replacing cabinets and counters alone doesn't need a permit.
  • New Castle County, DE — Cabinet and counter replacement doesn't need a permit.
Kitchen Remodels questions
Good to know.
Do I need a permit to redo my kitchen?

Cabinets and counters alone often don't (Tredyffrin and New Castle County both exempt them). Moving plumbing, electrical or walls does. We tell you which applies.

Can you work around older walls that aren't square?

Yes. That's finish carpentry: cabinets and trim are scribed to the wall so the gaps disappear.

Do you give written estimates?

Yes. You get a written scope and estimate before any work starts.
