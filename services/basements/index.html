How we build it

Basement Finishing, done the way it should be.

A finished basement is only as good as what's behind the walls. We frame and insulate against the foundation properly, plan the lighting and outlets before the drywall goes up, and make sure any bedroom has a legal way out.

Permits & approvals
The rules change town to town.

We pull the permits and handle historic review. A few rules that catch homeowners out:

  • Easttown — Finishing a basement needs a building permit.
  • Moorestown — A finished basement needs zoning review and plans from an NJ design professional.
  • Harrison Twp, NJ — The township's basement checklist covers egress: 5 sq ft opening, 44" maximum sill height.
  • South Jersey septic homes — Adding an ejector pump for a bathroom goes through the county health department.
Basement Finishing questions
Good to know.
Do I need a permit to finish my basement?

In almost every town we serve, yes. Easttown and Moorestown both require one. We pull it and schedule the inspections.

Can a basement bedroom be legal?

Yes, with a code-sized egress window or door. In Harrison Township that means at least 5 sq ft of opening with the sill no higher than 44 inches.

Who draws the plans in New Jersey?

New Jersey home improvement contractors can't prepare construction plans, so where plans are required we work with an NJ design professional.

Can you add a bathroom?

Yes. On septic homes in South Jersey the ejector pump goes through the county health department, which we plan for.
